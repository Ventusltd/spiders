The machine-readable [NEXT-50.json](NEXT-50.json) gives module paths and concrete proof requirements. It is the build queue; P0?P9 remain cross-cutting issues. Unchanged direct first-pass geometry remains available through every route or installation alternative. Existing producer baselines are immutable.

The Cable owner migration is first. Escape handling and focus containment are separate host increments. Standalone tool printing has its own proof stages; Atlas/Pipeline print passes do not cover it. Civil construction models, electrical studies, requirements, professional review and finance remain separately scoped. The final compiler must retain genuine failing required fleet gates.

## Finding the relationships behind the picture

The Kuiper learning work makes an address and its calculated position visible. Spiders documents another part of understanding a system: the relationships between repositories and the network-topology views used to explore them.

A computed position and a connection describe different things. Nearby points in a Kuiper drawing do not, by themselves, establish an electrical connection. Relationship data must come from the relevant source and retain its provenance.

This diary connects the public learning experience, geometry, mathematical notes and regression-check work so that a reader can follow each subject to its owner. It adds documentation links; it does not introduce a new Spider species or claim a new engineering integration.

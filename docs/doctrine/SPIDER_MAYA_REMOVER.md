# Spider Maya Remover

Maya here means false appearance.

It is the thing that makes a broken surface look healthy.

It is the thing that makes an unknown node look settled.

It is the thing that makes a derived guess look declared.

The Spider removes maya by refusing false certainty.

It does not hide degraded states.

It does not cover missing evidence.

It does not paint fake green.

It records unknown as unknown.

It records degraded as degraded.

It records declared as declared.

It records derived as derived.

This is not only philosophy.

It is interface behaviour.

A grey card is truth when the Spider has not proved green.

A failed fetch is truth when the source cannot be reached.

A missing child scope is truth when no committed cartridge exists.

A recovery copy is truth when the live surface has become unstable.

The Spider is useful because it can show what is not yet known.

That is the removal of maya.

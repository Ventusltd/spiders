# Spider Senses

A Spider sees through named organs.

An eye is a rendering surface.

It shows shape, place, path, relation or topology.

A map view is an eye.

A radial graph is an eye.

A dependency web is an eye.

A sense is a signal surface.

It notices motion, change, absence or degradation.

A source is where signal or fact comes from.

A source may be a public dataset, a committed file, a manifest, a live URL, a report or a registry.

A surface is where a human can see the Spider output.

A surface may be a dashboard, static page, report or species page.

A cartridge is a small committed payload that lets a Spider load a child scope without inventing it in the renderer.

A degraded surface is not a failure to hide.

It is a fact to chart.

The Spider produces maps, child scopes, status views, recovery copies and receipts.

It does not produce certainty from nothing.

The smell-see-chart loop is the basic motion.

Smell means detect a signal.

See means resolve the signal into an observed state.

Chart means render the state with provenance and boundary.

# Spider OS Rules

Document type: doctrine

Status: draft operating law

## Purpose

Spider OS defines how Spider species scan, present and build without losing truth discipline.

The OS exists so the project can build large and detailed power-system and federation maps without every new repo relearning the same rules.

## Core roles

The federation repo is the governed map and hibernation chamber for the first Spider.

The spiders repo is the species lab.

The monolith is the historical working body and source archive.

Data repos are clean proof and data stores.

## Prime laws

The Spider may observe widely.

The Spider must claim narrowly.

The Spider may derive findings.

The Spider must not promote derived findings into declared truth.

The Spider may screen.

The Spider must not certify unless a future professional verification layer explicitly carries that authority.

The renderer reads graph data.

The renderer does not author graph truth.

## Provenance law

Every important node, edge, source, file, document and display state must be traceable.

Allowed provenance states are:

```text
declared
derived
unknown
```

Declared means a source, standard, repository, principal instruction or committed artefact explicitly states the fact.

Derived means a scanner, workflow, model or human analysis inferred the fact from observed material.

Unknown means the Spider has not yet proved the state.

## Human dispatch law

A derived finding may recommend action.

A human must promote it before it becomes declared truth.

No automated workflow may silently promote derived findings into declared facts.

## True Corona law

The Spider may increase attention toward stranded, neglected, weakly connected, delayed, degraded or underserved nodes.

This may change visibility and ranking.

It must not change provenance or confidence.

## Failure law

A broken link, stale page, failed fetch or missing cartridge is not hidden.

It is charted as degraded, failed or unknown.

## Recovery law

Every major working surface needs a versioned recovery copy.

A screenshot is evidence of behaviour, not source recovery.
